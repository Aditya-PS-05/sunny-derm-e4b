Sunny model third-party notices
=================================

PAD-UFES-20 training images
---------------------------

Sunny PAD SmolVLM 500M was fine-tuned using 2,292 accepted smartphone
clinical images from PAD-UFES-20. The source dataset contains 2,298 images;
six images were excluded because the automated feature extractor could not
identify a usable lesion region.

Dataset: PAD-UFES-20: a skin lesion dataset composed of patient data and
clinical images collected from smartphones
Creators: Andre G. C. Pacheco, Gustavo R. Lima, Amanda S. Salomao,
Breno A. Krohling, Igor P. Biral, Gabriel G. de Angelo, Fabio C. R. Alves Jr,
Jose G. M. Esgario, Alana C. Simora, Pedro B. C. Castro,
Felipe B. Rodrigues, Patricia H. L. Frasson, Renato A. Krohling,
Helder Knidel, Maria C. S. Santos, Rachel B. Espirito Santo,
Telma L. S. G. Macedo, Tania R. P. Canuto, and Luiz F. S. de Barros.
Source: https://doi.org/10.17632/zr7vgbcyr2.1
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License link: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made: Sunny resized and normalized the images, generated
non-diagnostic appearance descriptions, split records by lesion for training
and validation, fine-tuned SmolVLM 500M with a LoRA adapter, merged the adapter,
converted the result to GGUF, and quantized the language model and vision
projector for deployment. The original creators and institutions do not
endorse Sunny.

SmolVLM 500M
-------------

Sunny PAD SmolVLM 500M is a modified model derived from
HuggingFaceTB/SmolVLM-500M-Instruct. SmolVLM is provided by Hugging Face under
the Apache License, Version 2.0.
Source: https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolVLM-500M-Instruct
License: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Sunny modified SmolVLM 500M by fine-tuning it on PAD-UFES-20-derived
non-diagnostic description records, merging the adapter into the base weights,
and converting and quantizing the result to GGUF. Hugging Face does not endorse
Sunny. A complete copy of the Apache License 2.0 accompanies this notice as
Apache-2.0.txt.
